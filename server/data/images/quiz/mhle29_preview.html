Task 5 · Application of measurement in real situations.

1) A can of 14 l of oil was purchased for an event. After the event, 6 l 325 ml of oil was left in the can. How much oil was used for the event?

Answer =
Think about: Total oil purchased = 14 l
✅ Solution
Total oil purchased = 14 l. Oil left = 6 l 325 ml. Oil used = Total oil - Oil left. Convert all to milliliters for easy subtraction: 14 l = 14 × 1000 ml = 14000 ml. 6 l 325 ml = (6 × 1000 ml) + 325 ml = 6000 ml + 325 ml = 6325 ml. Oil used = 14000 ml - 6325 ml = 7675 ml. Convert back to liters and milliliters: 7675 ml = 7 l 675 ml.

Answer =
Think about: Calculate the total volume of juice from three bottles:
✅ Solution
