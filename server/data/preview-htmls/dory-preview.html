MUST DO

Task 1 · Character Map of Story

a) Draw a character map for the main character in the story. Include sections for: Appearance, Actions, and What she says.

This is a hands-on activity. Try it on paper!

Think about how the character looks, what they do, and what they say in the story.
✅ Solution
1. Draw a large circle or box for the character. 2. Divide it into three sections: Appearance, Actions, What she says. 3. Fill in details from the story (e.g., 'bunchy hedge' for appearance, 'galloping' for actions).
MUST DO

Task 2 · Describe Her

a) Describe her appearance using adjectives.
Look at the words near 'appearance' in the worksheet. Use describing words like 'bunchy' or 'tall'.
✅ 1. Read the story to find details about the character's looks. 2. Write adjectives that describe her (e.g., 'She has a bunchy hedge for hair').
c) Write what she says in the story.
Find the part of the story where the character speaks. Write her exact words or summarize them.
✅ 1. Read the story to find the character's dialogue. 2. Write down what she says (e.g., 'I am going to the market').
b) Describe her actions using verbs.
Look at the words near 'actions' in the worksheet. Use action words like 'galloping'.
✅ 1. Read the story to find what the character does. 2. Write verbs that describe her actions (e.g., 'She is galloping').
d) Does she have a gross or kind personality? Explain your answer.
Think about how the character treats others in the story. Use details from her actions and words.
✅ 1. Read the story to see how the character behaves. 2. Decide if she is gross (mean) or kind (nice). 3. Give reasons for your answer (e.g., 'She is kind because she shares her food').
MUST DO

Task 3 · Dive into the Dictionary!

a) Look up the word bunchy in the dictionary. Write its meaning.

Answer =
Use a dictionary to find the word. Read the definition carefully.
✅ Solution
1. Open the dictionary to the letter 'B'. 2. Find the word 'bunchy'. 3. Read and write down its meaning.

b) Look up the word galloping in the dictionary. Write its meaning and use it in a sentence.

Answer =
Find the word in the dictionary. Write its meaning and then create a sentence with it.
✅ Solution
1. Open the dictionary to the letter 'G'. 2. Find the word 'galloping'. 3. Write its meaning. 4. Use it in a sentence (e.g., 'The child was galloping like a horse').
COULD DO

Task 4 · Adjectives

a) List 5 adjectives that describe the character's appearance from the story.

Answer =
Think about how the character looks. Use words like 'bunchy' or 'tall'.
✅ Solution
1. Read the story to find details about the character's appearance. 2. Write 5 describing words (e.g., 'bunchy', 'tall', 'curly', 'bright', 'small').

b) List 5 adjectives that describe the character's personality from the story.

Answer =
Think about how the character behaves. Use words like 'kind' or 'brave'.
✅ Solution
1. Read the story to find details about the character's actions and words. 2. Write 5 describing words (e.g., 'kind', 'brave', 'funny', 'gross', 'shy').
ENRICHMENT

Task 5 · Creative Writing

a) Write a short paragraph (5-6 sentences) describing a new adventure for the character. Use at least 3 adjectives and 2 action words.

This is a hands-on activity. Try it on paper!

Think about where the character could go next. Use words like 'bunchy', 'galloping', or 'kind'.
✅ Solution
1. Imagine a new adventure for the character (e.g., 'She went to a magical forest'). 2. Use adjectives to describe things (e.g., 'bunchy trees', 'bright flowers'). 3. Use action words (e.g., 'galloping', 'jumping'). 4. Write 5-6 sentences.
