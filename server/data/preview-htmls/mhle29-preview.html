Task 2 · Reduce the fractions to their lowest terms.

a) 30/40 = ___

Answer =
Find the greatest common factor (GCF) of 30 and 40. Divide both numerator and denominator by the GCF.
✅ Solution
GCF of 30 and 40 is 10. 30 ÷ 10 = 3; 40 ÷ 10 = 4. So, 30/40 simplifies to 3/4.

Task 3 · Solve the following.

a) 40 l 300 ml + 12 l 985 ml = ___ l ___ ml

Answer = l, ml
Add the litres and millilitres separately. If millilitres exceed 1000, convert to litres.
✅ Solution
40 l + 12 l = 52 l; 300 ml + 985 ml = 1285 ml. 1285 ml = 1 l 285 ml. So, 52 l + 1 l = 53 l and 285 ml remains. Total: 53 l 285 ml.

b) 21200 ml - 11300 ml = ___ ml

Answer = ml
Subtract the two amounts directly since both are in millilitres.
✅ Solution
21200 ml - 11300 ml = 9900 ml

Task 4 · Compare fractions with < or > or = in the box.

a) 7/8 ___ 6/8

Answer =
Compare the numerators when denominators are the same. The larger numerator means a larger fraction.
✅ Solution
Both fractions have the same denominator (8). 7 > 6, so 7/8 > 6/8.

b) 1/8 ___ 9/8

Answer =
Compare the numerators when denominators are the same. The smaller numerator means a smaller fraction.
✅ Solution
Both fractions have the same denominator (8). 1 < 9, so 1/8 < 9/8.

c) 1/12 ___ 7/12

Answer =
Compare the numerators when denominators are the same. The smaller numerator means a smaller fraction.
✅ Solution
Both fractions have the same denominator (12). 1 < 7, so 1/12 < 7/12.

Task 5 · Application of measurement in real situations.

a) A can of 14 l of oil was purchased for an event. After the event, 6 l 325 ml of oil was left in the can. How much oil was used for the event?

Answer = l, ml
Subtract the remaining oil from the total oil purchased. Convert litres to millilitres if needed.
✅ Solution
Total oil: 14 l = 14000 ml; Remaining oil: 6 l 325 ml = 6325 ml. Oil used = 14000 ml - 6325 ml = 7675 ml = 7 l 675 ml.
