Task 2 · Build a decimal pyramid.

1) Find the missing decimal at the top of the first pyramid.

Answer =
Add the two bottom blocks: 3.3 + 1.2 = 4.5. Then add the next block: 4.5 + 0.1 = 4.6.
✅ Solution
The bottom row is 3.3, 1.2, and 0.1. The middle block is 3.3 + 1.2 = 4.5. The top block is 4.5 + 0.1 = 4.6.

✅ Solution

Task 5 · Decimals in real life. Solve the following.

1a) You have Rs. 695. You buy a book for Rs. 123.55 and a crayon box for Rs. 169.75. How much did you spend in total?

Answer = Rs.
Add the cost of the book and the crayon box.
✅ Solution
123.55 + 169.75 = 293.30. Total spent is Rs. 293.30.

1b) How much money is left with you?

Answer = Rs.
Subtract the total spent from the total money you have.
✅ Solution
695.00 - 293.30 = 401.70. Money left is Rs. 401.70.

2) A garden has an irregular shape with sides measuring 19.2 m, 18.3 m, 14.9 m, and 24.4 m. What is the total length of its boundary? Express the total length in centimetres.

Side = cm
Add all the side lengths in metres, then convert to centimetres by multiplying by 100.
✅ Solution
19.2 + 18.3 + 14.9 + 24.4 = 76.8 metres. 76.8 × 100 = 7680 cm.

3) The cost of 1 kg rice is Rs. 58.25. If a family needs 30 kgs, how much money will they spend?

Answer = Rs.
Multiply the cost per kg by the number of kgs needed.
✅ Solution
58.25 × 30 = 1747.50. Total cost is Rs. 1747.50.

4) Subtract 135.00 from the sum of 76.30 and 44.85.

Answer =
First add 76.30 and 44.85, then subtract 135.00.
✅ Solution
76.30 + 44.85 = 121.15. 121.15 - 135.00 = -13.85.
