Task 1 · Read each riddle carefully. Choose the correct answer from the word bank above.

1) I am a place where people come to buy and sell many things.

Answer =
Think of a place like a shopping area or bazaar.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'market' because it is a place where buying and selling occur.

2) I am a person who buys things from shops or markets.

Answer =
This person purchases goods or services.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'buyer' because a buyer is someone who purchases items.

3) I am the person who sells goods to others.

Answer =
This person offers goods or services for sale.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'seller' because a seller is someone who sells items.

4) I am the process of making goods using materials and effort.

Answer =
This involves creating items from raw materials.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'production' because it refers to the process of making goods.

5) I am what happens when people use or buy goods and services.

Answer =
This is the act of using or purchasing goods and services.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'consumption' because it refers to the use of goods and services.

6) I am things like water, soil, and minerals that help us make products.

Answer =
These are natural materials used to produce goods.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'resources' because they are inputs used in production.

7) I am items you can touch and buy, like books, toys, or fruits.

Answer =
These are physical items that can be purchased.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'goods' because they are tangible items for sale.

8) I am something you cannot touch but can use, like teaching or transport.

Answer =
These are activities provided by others for a fee.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'services' because they are intangible activities provided to consumers.

9) I am the buying and selling of goods between people or countries.

Answer =
This involves exchanging goods and services.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'trade' because it refers to the exchange of goods and services.

10) I am a system that shows how people produce, buy, and sell things in a place.

Answer =
This is the overall system of production and consumption in a region.
✅ Solution
The correct word is 'economy' because it refers to the system of production, distribution, and consumption.

Task 3 · Error Detective Alert - Read & Detect Errors

In a small town, there was a busy buyer where people came every day. Farmers were involved in consumption, growing crops using natural goods like water and soil. Customers visited the town for production, buying things they needed. A seller would choose items carefully, while a market tried to offer the best price. This exchange of goods and services kept the town’s trade active and growing.

a) Identify and correct the incorrect word: buyer

Think about where people come to buy and sell things.
✅ Solution
The incorrect word is 'buyer'. The correct word is 'market' because a market is a place where buying and selling occur.

b) Identify and correct the incorrect word: consumption

Think about what farmers do when they grow crops.
✅ Solution
The incorrect word is 'consumption'. The correct word is 'production' because farmers produce crops.

c) Identify and correct the incorrect word: goods

Think about what water and soil are called in economic terms.
✅ Solution
The incorrect word is 'goods'. The correct word is 'resources' because water and soil are natural resources.

d) Identify and correct the incorrect word: production

Think about what customers do when they buy things.
✅ Solution
The incorrect word is 'production'. The correct word is 'consumption' because customers consume goods and services.

e) Identify and correct the incorrect word: market

Think about who chooses items carefully in a market.
✅ Solution
The incorrect word is 'market'. The correct word is 'seller' because a seller chooses items to sell.
