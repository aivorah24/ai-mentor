Personal Narrative

A true story about something that happened to you. It includes details about where you went, who you were with, what you did, and how you felt.

📚

Brainstorming

Thinking and writing down ideas before you start writing a story. It helps you remember all the important parts.

📚

Hamburger Strategy

A way to write a story with a clear beginning, middle, and end. The top bun is the introduction, the fillings are the details, and the bottom bun is the conclusion.

📝 Note

Purpose: To be able to write and explore personal narratives. Learning Outcome: Learner inquires about a place one plans to visit and clearly describes his/her experiences.

🎯 Success Criteria

  • I can write about different moments from my holiday.
  • I can describe where I went, who I spent time with, my favourite moment, fun things I did, challenges I faced, and how I felt.
  • I can use my brainstorming ideas to write a personal narrative in clear paragraphs using the hamburger strategy.
MUST DO

Task 1 · Write down the different moments from your holiday in the circles below

a) Where did you go?

Think about the city, country, or place you visited during your holiday.
✅ Solution
Write the name of the place you visited. For example: 'I went to Goa' or 'I visited my grandmother in Delhi.'

b) Who did you spend time with?

Think about your family, friends, or relatives you were with during the holiday.
✅ Solution
Write the names of the people you spent time with. For example: 'I spent time with my parents and my sister' or 'I was with my cousins.'

c) What was your favourite moment?

Think about the best part of your holiday. It could be an activity, a place, or a special event.
✅ Solution
Describe your favourite moment in detail. For example: 'My favourite moment was when we went to the beach and built a sandcastle' or 'I loved eating ice cream with my brother.'

d) What fun things did you do?

Think about the activities you enjoyed during your holiday. Did you play games, visit places, or try new things?
✅ Solution
List or describe the fun activities you did. For example: 'I played cricket with my cousins' or 'I went to a museum and saw dinosaur bones.'

e) Did you face any challenges? What were they?

Think about things that were difficult or not fun during your holiday. Did you miss home, get lost, or have any problems?
✅ Solution
Describe any challenges you faced. For example: 'I missed my friends from school' or 'I got a little scared on the airplane.'

f) How did you feel?

Think about your emotions during the holiday. Were you happy, excited, sad, or bored?
✅ Solution
Describe how you felt during different moments of your holiday. For example: 'I felt happy when I was at the beach' or 'I felt excited when I saw snow for the first time.'
MUST DO

Task 2 · Write a personal narrative about a place you visited or will visit during the Christmas holidays

a) Write a personal narrative using the brainstorming circles to help you with your writing. Remember to use the hamburger strategy and write in clear paragraphs.

Start with an introduction (where did you go and who were you with?). Then write the details (what did you do, what was your favourite moment, how did you feel?). End with a conclusion (what did you learn or why was this trip special?).
✅ Solution
Use the ideas from Task 1 to write your story. For example: Paragraph 1 (Introduction): 'During the Christmas holidays, I went to Shimla with my family. We had so much fun together!' Paragraph 2 (Details): 'My favourite moment was when we built a snowman. I also loved drinking hot chocolate and playing in the snow with my sister. It was cold, but I felt very happy.' Paragraph 3 (Conclusion): 'I will never forget this trip because it was my first time seeing snow!'
