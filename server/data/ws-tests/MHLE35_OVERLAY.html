Task 5 · Solve the following.

1a) You have Rs. 695. You buy a book for Rs.123.55 and a crayon box for 169.75. How much did you spend in total?

Answer = Rs.
Add the cost of the book and the crayon box.
✅ Solution
Total spent = Cost of book + Cost of crayon box = Rs. 123.55 + Rs. 169.75 = Rs. 293.30.

1b) You have Rs. 695. You buy a book for Rs.123.55 and a crayon box for 169.75. How much money is left with you?

Answer = Rs.
Subtract the total money spent from the initial amount you had.
✅ Solution
Money left = Initial amount - Total spent = Rs. 695.00 - Rs. 293.30 = Rs. 401.70.

2) A garden has an irregular shape with sides measuring 19.2 m, 18.3 m, 14.9 m, and 24.4 m. What is the total length of its boundary? Express the total length in centimetres.

Side = cm
First, find the sum of all sides in meters. Then, convert meters to centimeters (1 m = 100 cm).
✅ Solution
Total length in meters = 19.2 + 18.3 + 14.9 + 24.4 = 76.8 m. Convert to centimeters: 76.8 m * 100 cm/m = 7680 cm.

3) The cost of 1 kg rice is Rs.58.25. If a family needs 30 kgs, how much money will they spend?

Answer = Rs.
Multiply the cost of 1 kg by the total number of kgs needed.
✅ Solution
Total cost = Cost per kg * Quantity = Rs. 58.25 * 30 = Rs. 1747.50.

4) Subtract 135.00 from the sum of 76.30 and 44.85.

Answer =
First, find the sum of 76.30 and 44.85. Then, subtract 135.00 from that sum.
✅ Solution
Sum of 76.30 and 44.85 = 76.30 + 44.85 = 121.15. Subtract 135.00 from the sum: 121.15 - 135.00 = -13.85.
