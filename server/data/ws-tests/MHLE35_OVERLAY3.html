Q17. Pyramid 3: Bottom-middle block

Answer =
For Pyramid 3, let the unknown bottom-middle block be 'X'. The two middle blocks will be (3.6 + X) and (X + 2.9). Their sum equals the top block (4.7). Set up an equation: (3.6 + X) + (X + 2.9) = 4.7.
✅ Solution
Pyramid 3: Let the bottom-middle block be 'X'. Middle-left block = 3.6 + X Middle-right block = X + 2.9 Top block = (3.6 + X) + (X + 2.9) = 4.7 6.5 + 2X = 4.7 2X = 4.7 - 6.5 2X = -1.8 X = -0.9 So, the bottom-middle block is -0.9.

Task 5 · Solve the following.

1a) You have Rs. 695. You buy a book for Rs.123.55 and a crayon box for Rs.169.75. How much did you spend in total?

Answer = Rs.
To find the total amount spent, add the cost of the book and the crayon box.
✅ Solution
Cost of book = Rs.123.55 Cost of crayon box = Rs.169.75 Total spent = 123.55 + 169.75 = Rs.293.30

1b) How much money is left with you?

Answer = Rs.
Subtract the total amount spent from the initial amount of money you had.
✅ Solution
Initial money = Rs.695.00 Total spent = Rs.293.30 Money left = 695.00 - 293.30 = Rs.401.70

2) A garden has an irregular shape with sides measuring 19.2 m, 18.3 m, 14.9 m, and 24.4 m. What is the total length of its boundary? Express the total length in centimetres.

Side = cm
First, find the total length of the boundary by adding all the side lengths in meters. Then, convert the total length from meters to centimeters (1 meter = 100 centimeters).
✅ Solution
Total length in meters = 19.2 + 18.3 + 14.9 + 24.4 = 76.8 meters. To convert meters to centimeters, multiply by 100. 76.8 m * 100 = 7680 cm.

3) The cost of 1 kg rice is Rs.58.25. If a family needs 30 kgs, how much money will they spend?

Answer = Rs.
To find the total cost, multiply the cost of 1 kg by the total number of kgs needed.
✅ Solution
Cost of 1 kg rice = Rs.58.25 Quantity needed = 30 kgs Total money spent = 58.25 * 30 = Rs.1747.50

4) Subtract 135.00 from the sum of 76.30 and 44.85.

Answer =
First, find the sum of 76.30 and 44.85. Then, subtract 135.00 from that sum.
✅ Solution
Sum of 76.30 and 44.85 = 76.30 + 44.85 = 121.15 Subtract 135.00 from the sum: 121.15 - 135.00 = -13.85
