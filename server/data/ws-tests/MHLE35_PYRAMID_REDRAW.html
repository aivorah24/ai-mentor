Task 1 · Express the given decimals as fractions.

a) a) 0.76

Answer =
Decimals with two places after the point are expressed over 100. Simplify the fraction.
✅ Solution
0.76 = 76/100. Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 4. 76 ÷ 4 = 19, 100 ÷ 4 = 25. So, 76/100 = 19/25.

b) b)0.05

Answer =
Decimals with two places after the point are expressed over 100. Simplify the fraction.
✅ Solution
0.05 = 5/100. Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 5. 5 ÷ 5 = 1, 100 ÷ 5 = 20. So, 5/100 = 1/20.

c) c)1.3

Answer =
Decimals with one place after the point are expressed over 10. Convert mixed number to an improper fraction.
✅ Solution
1.3 is equivalent to 1 and 3/10. To express as an improper fraction: (1 × 10 + 3) / 10 = 13/10.

d) d)2.8

Answer =
Decimals with one place after the point are expressed over 10. Simplify the improper fraction.
✅ Solution
2.8 is equivalent to 2 and 8/10. To express as an improper fraction: (2 × 10 + 8) / 10 = 28/10. Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 2. 28 ÷ 2 = 14, 10 ÷ 2 = 5. So, 28/10 = 14/5.

e) e)6.44

Answer =
Decimals with two places after the point are expressed over 100. Simplify the improper fraction.
✅ Solution
6.44 is equivalent to 6 and 44/100. To express as an improper fraction: (6 × 100 + 44) / 100 = 644/100. Divide both the numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor, which is 4. 644 ÷ 4 = 161, 100 ÷ 4 = 25. So, 644/100 = 161/25.

a) a) 16 hrs. = minutesminutes

✅ 1 hour = 60 minutes. So, 16 hours × 60 minutes/hour = 960 minutes.

Task 5 · Solve the following.

1a) 1. You have Rs. 695. You buy a book for Rs.123.55 and a crayon box for 169.75. 1. How much did you spend in total?

Answer = Rs.
Add the cost of the book and the crayon box to find the total amount spent.
✅ Solution
Cost of book = Rs.123.55. Cost of crayon box = Rs.169.75. Total spent = 123.55 + 169.75 = Rs.293.30.

1b) b)How much money is left with you?

Answer = Rs.
Subtract the total amount spent (calculated in part a) from the initial money you had.
✅ Solution
Initial money = Rs.695.00. Total spent = Rs.293.30. Money left = 695.00 - 293.30 = Rs.401.70.

2) 2) A garden has an irregular shape with sides measuring 19.2 m, 18.3 m, 14.9 m, and 24.4 m. What is the total length of its boundary? Express the total length in centimetres.

Side = cm
First, add all the side lengths in meters to find the perimeter. Then convert the total meters to centimeters (1 m = 100 cm).
✅ Solution
Total length in meters = 19.2 + 18.3 + 14.9 + 24.4 = 76.8 m. To convert to centimeters, multiply by 100: 76.8 × 100 = 7680 cm.

3) 3) The cost of 1 kg rice is Rs.58.25. If a family needs 30 kgs, how much money will they spend?

Answer = Rs.
Multiply the cost per kg by the total number of kgs needed.
✅ Solution
Cost of 1 kg rice = Rs.58.25. Quantity needed = 30 kgs. Total money spent = 58.25 × 30 = Rs.1747.50.

4) 4) Subtract 135.00 from the sum of 76.30 and 44.85.

Answer =
First, find the sum of 76.30 and 44.85. Then, subtract 135.00 from that sum.
✅ Solution
Sum of 76.30 and 44.85 = 76.30 + 44.85 = 121.15. Subtract 135.00 from the sum: 121.15 - 135.00 = -13.85.
