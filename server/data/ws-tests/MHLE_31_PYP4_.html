Task 4 · Find the area and perimeter of the given squares with the following dimensions.

Diagram for this task could not be auto-cropped from the worksheet.
4a) A square has a side of 6 cm. Find its Perimeter and Area.
For a square, Perimeter = 4 × side and Area = side × side.
✅ Given side = 6 cm. Perimeter = 4 × 6 cm = 24 cm. Area = 6 cm × 6 cm = 36 sq. cm.
4b) A square has an area of 100 sq. cm. Find its side length and Perimeter.
To find the side from the area, calculate the square root of the area. Then use the side length to find the perimeter.
✅ Given Area = 100 sq. cm. Side × Side = 100 sq. cm, so Side = √100 = 10 cm. Perimeter = 4 × Side = 4 × 10 cm = 40 cm.

Enrichment Task: Relationship of area and perimeter. Draw a square with the same area and perimeter. Label the sides.

5a) Draw a square with the same area and perimeter. Label the sides.

This is a hands-on activity. Try it on paper!

Let the side of the square be 's'. Set up an equation where Area = Perimeter and solve for 's'. (s × s = 4 × s)
✅ Solution
Let the side of the square be 's'. Area = s × s = s² Perimeter = 4 × s For Area to be equal to Perimeter: s² = 4s Divide both sides by 's' (assuming s ≠ 0): s = 4. So, a square with side length 4 units will have Area = 4 × 4 = 16 sq. units and Perimeter = 4 × 4 = 16 units.
