Task 2 · Find the perimeter of the L shaped polygon.

1) Find the perimeter of the L shaped polygon.

Perimeter = m
First, identify the lengths of the two missing inner sides by using the given outer dimensions. Then, sum up all outer side lengths.
✅ Solution
The given outer sides are 6 m, 5 m, 8 m, and 3 m. To find the perimeter, we need to determine the lengths of the two inner sides: 1. The inner horizontal side: The total bottom horizontal length is 8 m, and the top horizontal segment is 5 m. So, the inner horizontal segment is 8 m - 5 m = 3 m. 2. The inner vertical side: The total left vertical length is 6 m, and the exposed right vertical segment is 3 m. So, the inner vertical segment is 6 m - 3 m = 3 m. Perimeter = Sum of all outer sides = 6 m + 5 m + 3 m + 3 m + 8 m + 3 m = 28 m.

Task 3 · Application of area and perimeter in real situations.

1) A square playground has sides that are 65 m long. What is the area of the playground?

Side = m²
The area of a square is calculated by multiplying its side length by itself (side × side).
✅ Solution
Area of a square playground = Side × Side. Given side = 65 m. Area = 65 m × 65 m = 4225 m².

2) Amit jogged four times around the park. If the perimeter of the park is 450 m, how far did he jog? Give your answer in km and m.

Perimeter = km m
First, calculate the total distance in meters (Perimeter × Number of rounds). Then, convert meters to kilometers and meters (1 km = 1000 m).
✅ Solution
Total distance jogged = Perimeter of the park × Number of times jogged. Total distance = 450 m × 4 = 1800 m. To convert meters to kilometers and meters: 1 km = 1000 m. So, 1800 m = 1000 m + 800 m = 1 km 800 m.

3) The length and breadth of a rectangular park is 200 m and 250 m respectively. If a person takes 5 rounds around the park, how much did he walk?

Distance = m
First, calculate the perimeter of the rectangular park using the formula 2 × (Length + Breadth). Then, multiply the perimeter by the number of rounds.
✅ Solution
Perimeter of a rectangular park = 2 × (Length + Breadth). Perimeter = 2 × (200 m + 250 m) = 2 × 450 m = 900 m. Total distance walked = Perimeter × Number of rounds. Total distance = 900 m × 5 = 4500 m.

Enrichment Task:

1) A rectangular room has a length of 120 m and a breadth of 80 m. The floor is to be completely covered with square tiles of side 40 cm. Find the number of tiles required to cover the floor.

Side = tiles
Convert all measurements to the same unit (e.g., centimeters). Calculate the area of the room and the area of one tile. Divide the room's area by the tile's area.
✅ Solution
First, ensure all units are consistent. Convert room dimensions from meters to centimeters: Room Length = 120 m = 120 × 100 cm = 12000 cm. Room Breadth = 80 m = 80 × 100 cm = 8000 cm. Area of the room = Length × Breadth = 12000 cm × 8000 cm = 96,000,000 cm². Area of one square tile = Side × Side = 40 cm × 40 cm = 1600 cm². Number of tiles required = Area of the room / Area of one tile = 96,000,000 cm² / 1600 cm² = 60,000 tiles.
