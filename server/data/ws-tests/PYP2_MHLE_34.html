✅ Solution
Human weight is measured in kilograms. Grams are used for very light objects.

3) A table is about 1 long.

Is a table usually shorter or longer than 1 centimeter?
✅ Solution
A table is a relatively large piece of furniture, often around 1 meter or more in length. 1 centimetre is tiny, like the width of a fingernail.

4) A chocolate bar weighs about 50 .

How heavy is a chocolate bar compared to a bag of sugar?
✅ Solution
A chocolate bar is a small item. 50 kilograms is the weight of a person. 50 grams is a reasonable weight for a single chocolate bar.

Remember that 1 kilogram equals 1000 grams.
✅ Solution
1 kg is equal to 1000 g. Comparing 1000 g and 500 g, 1000 g is heavier.

Remember that 1 meter equals 100 centimetres.
✅ Solution

✅ Solution

Answer = centimetres
Use a ruler to carefully measure the length of line segment CD in centimetres.
✅ Solution
Using a ruler, measure the distance between point C and point D. A typical measurement for this line segment would be approximately 4.5 cm.

Answer = centimetres
Subtract the length of CD from the length of AB to find the difference.
✅ Solution

ENRICHMENT

Enrichment Task: c) All About Time

1) Your school starts at 8:30. What time is it half an hour later?

Answer =
Half an hour is 30 minutes. Add 30 minutes to 8:30.
✅ Solution
If school starts at 8:30, half an hour later means adding 30 minutes. 8:30 + 30 minutes = 9:00.

2) If a movie starts at 5:00 and ends at 6:00, how long is it?

Answer = hour
Subtract the start time from the end time to find the duration.
✅ Solution
The movie starts at 5:00 and ends at 6:00. The duration of the movie is 6:00 - 5:00 = 1 hour.
