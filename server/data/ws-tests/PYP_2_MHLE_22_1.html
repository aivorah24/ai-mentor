Task 2 · Solve the following word problems using the appropriate number operation.

a) There are 507 crows in the field. A loud sound scares 224 of them away. How many crows are left?

Answer = crows
When some items are 'scared away' or 'left', it usually means you need to subtract.
✅ Solution
Total crows = 507 Crows scared away = 224 Crows left = Total crows - Crows scared away = 507 - 224 507 - 224 ------ 283 There are 283 crows left.

b) A market sells 476 apples in one week and 348 apples in the next week. How many apples have been sold in total?

Answer = apples
The phrase 'in total' indicates an addition operation.
✅ Solution
Apples sold in first week = 476 Apples sold in next week = 348 Total apples sold = Apples in first week + Apples in next week = 476 + 348 476 + 348 ------ 824 824 apples have been sold in total.

a) Leena has a collection of 673 books. She gives 126 books to Meena and 258 books to Teena. How many books are left with her now?

Answer = books
First, find out the total number of books Leena gave away. Then, subtract that total from her initial collection.
✅ Solution
Total books Leena has = 673 Books given to Meena = 126 Books given to Teena = 258
Step 1: Calculate total books given away: 126 + 258 = 384 books
Step 2: Calculate books left with Leena: 673 - 384 = 289 books There are 289 books left with her now.

Task 4 · Identify the mistake. Write the correct working and explain the mistake.

a) Identify the mistake in the following subtraction. Write the correct working and explain the mistake. 203 - 57 ----- 373

Carefully review the regrouping process (borrowing) in subtraction, especially when a digit in the top number is smaller than the corresponding digit in the bottom number.
✅ Solution
The mistake is in the calculation as regrouping was not applied correctly. Correct Working: 1 9 13 2 0 3 - 5 7 ------- 1 4 6 Explanation of the Mistake: 1. In the ones place, 3 - 7 cannot be 3. You need to regroup from the tens place. Since the tens place is 0, you must regroup from the hundreds place. The 2 (hundreds) becomes 1. The 0 (tens) becomes 10. Then, you regroup from the tens place to the ones place. The 10 (tens) becomes 9, and the 3 (ones) becomes 13. 2. Now, in the ones place: 13 - 7 = 6. 3. In the tens place: 9 - 5 = 4. 4. In the hundreds place: 1 - 0 = 1. The student's answer of 373 indicates they did not regroup. For example, in the ones place, they might have incorrectly done 7-3=4 and then written 3. In the tens place, 0-5 would not be 7. They seem to have subtracted 5 from 10 (from 20) and added it to the hundreds, or just subtracted incorrectly without following place value and borrowing rules.

a) Make two 3-digit numbers from the numbers below [3, 5, 2, 4, 1, 0]. Use each number once. What are the two 3-digit numbers that give the greatest answer when you subtract them? Also, consider the fox's question: 'Which two digits can you subtract to get the greatest hundred?'

To maximize a subtraction result (A - B), make A as large as possible and B as small as possible using the given digits. For the fox's question, consider the largest possible difference between any two digits.
✅ Solution
1. To get the greatest answer from subtracting two 3-digit numbers (A - B), 'A' must be the largest possible 3-digit number, and 'B' must be the smallest possible 3-digit number, using each digit from [3, 5, 2, 4, 1, 0] exactly once. Largest 3-digit number (A) from {5,4,3,2,1,0}: Arrange the largest three digits in descending order: 543. Remaining digits: {2,1,0}. Smallest 3-digit number (B) from {2,1,0}: Arrange the smallest available digits (ensuring the hundreds digit is not zero) in ascending order: 102. Subtract: 543 - 102 = 441. The greatest answer is 441. 2. For the fox's question, 'Which two digits can you subtract to get the greatest hundred?': This is ambiguous. If it refers to the largest possible difference between any two single digits from the given set, it would be 5 - 0 = 5. If it refers to achieving the largest possible hundreds digit in the result of a subtraction, like in the previous problem where the hundreds digits 5 and 1 resulted in 4 in the hundreds place of the answer (441). Given the context of elementary math, 5-0=5 is the most straightforward interpretation for 'greatest difference'.
