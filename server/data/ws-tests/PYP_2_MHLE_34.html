Task 2 · Circle the correct answer.

1) What unit would you use to measure a book?

Is a book usually shorter or longer than a typical meter stick?
✅ Solution
Books are usually measured in centimetres because they are typically less than a meter long.

2) What unit would you use to measure your weight?

Do you weigh a few grams or many kilograms?
✅ Solution
People's weight is measured in kilograms because they are much heavier than just a few grams.

3) A table is about 1 (centimetre / metre) long.

Is a table usually shorter or longer than a typical meter stick?
✅ Solution
Most tables are about 1 meter long or more, not just 1 centimetre.

4) A chocolate bar weighs about 50 (gram / kilogram).

Is a chocolate bar heavy (kilograms) or relatively light (grams)?
✅ Solution
A chocolate bar is relatively light, so it is measured in grams. 50 kilograms would be extremely heavy for a chocolate bar.

5) Which is heavier? (1 kg of rice / 500 g of rice)

Remember that 1 kilogram (kg) is equal to 1000 grams (g).
✅ Solution
1 kg is equal to 1000 g. Since 1000 g is greater than 500 g, 1 kg of rice is heavier.

6) Which is longer? (1 m rope / 50 cm rope)

Remember that 1 metre (m) is equal to 100 centimetres (cm).
✅ Solution
1 m is equal to 100 cm. Since 100 cm is greater than 50 cm, 1 m rope is longer.

7) Which is lighter? (200 g / 1 kg)

Remember that 1 kilogram (kg) is equal to 1000 grams (g).
✅ Solution
1 kg is equal to 1000 g. Since 200 g is less than 1000 g, 200 g is lighter.

c) All About Time

1) Your school starts at 8:30. What time is it half an hour later?

Answer =
Half an hour is 30 minutes. Add 30 minutes to the start time.
✅ Solution
Starting at 8:30, adding half an hour (30 minutes) makes the time 8:30 + 0:30 = 9:00.

2) If a movie starts at 5:00 and ends at 6:00, how long is it?

Answer =
Find the difference between the end time and the start time.
✅ Solution
The movie ends at 6:00 and starts at 5:00. The duration is 6:00 - 5:00 = 1 hour.
