Task 1 · Bar Graph - Favourite Sport of Grade 4 Learners

1a) The scale on Y-axis is

Answer =
Look at the numbers on the vertical axis
✅ Solution
The Y-axis goes 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 so the scale is 2.

1b) The sum of votes for football and tennis is equal to the sum of votes for _____ and _____ sports.

Answer =
Add up the votes for football and tennis, then find two other sports with the same total
✅ Solution
The answer is: hockey and rugby

1c) Imagine the number of votes for rugby doubles. What will be its new height?
